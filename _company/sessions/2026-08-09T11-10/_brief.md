# 📋 작업 브리프

**원 명령:** 79.05 Zinc plates, sheets, strip and foil.

This heading covers plates, sheets, strip and foil, which are defined in Note 9 (d) to Section XV; these products correspond to the copper products described in the Explanatory Notes to headings 74.09 and 74.10.

Zinc plates and sheets are used for the manufacture of roofing tiles, dry battery cell containers, photo-engraving, lithographic or other printing plates, etc.

The heading does not cover :

(a) Expanded metal (heading 79.07).

(b) Prepared printing plates, etc., of heading 84.42.
